01 About me

Origin story

I grew up in Mauritius before it became what it is today: still underdeveloped and unknown.

I left to study finance in the Netherlands, found the money and missed the meaning, and joined the military to rebuild the discipline to go and look for it. Amsterdam showed me fashion from the inside, and Mr Marvis showed me a company can be built responsibly.

Everything since, the music, the design, the photographs, the talks and Force of Nature, comes from one instinct: create out of love, and others will feel it too.
